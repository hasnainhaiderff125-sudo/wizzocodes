How to Compare IPTV Subscription Plans

A practical checklist for comparing IPTV plans: what the numbers mean, what to ask, and how to avoid paying for more than you need.

By Wizzo Codes | Published 14 October 2026 | 2 min read

On this page

  • Look past the biggest number
  • Check the price and the currency
  • Confirm device support
  • Ask about support
  • Understand renewals, cancellations and refunds
  • Do not rely on promises
  • Start with the smallest sensible plan

Plans can look similar on the surface, so it helps to know what to compare and what to ask. Use this list before you decide, whoever the provider is.

Look past the biggest number

Plans often highlight totals for channels, films and series. Bigger numbers do not automatically mean a better fit. Ask whether the content you actually care about is included for viewers in the United Kingdom, and treat the totals as the provider's own figures rather than something independently checked.

Check the price and the currency

Make sure the price is clear, the currency is stated and the period is obvious. Our plans are priced in pounds sterling for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, with the total shown for the length you choose, and we confirm the price with you before you pay. See the current plans and prices.

Confirm device support

Check that your television, stick, phone or computer is supported. Use our compatibility checklist and send us your model. You can also request a free trial on WhatsApp to test channel quality and stability on your own device first.

Ask about support

Find out how you can reach support and when. Our plans include 24/7 WhatsApp support.

Understand renewals, cancellations and refunds

Ask how renewals or extensions work and read the refund policy before paying. Ours is on the refund and cancellation page.

Do not rely on promises

Be cautious of claims such as guaranteed uptime or the best service in the country. No provider can control your home network, and a claim without evidence is not a guarantee.

Start with the smallest sensible plan

If you are unsure, pick a plan that covers what you need now. You can ask about moving up later.
