Reinstalling an IPTV App Without Losing Access

How to reinstall an IPTV player app safely: keeping sign-in details, clearing old data, avoiding duplicate lists and checking stream limits.

By Wizzo Codes | Published 2 November 2026 | 2 min read

On this page

  • Keep your details
  • Clean reinstall
  • Avoid duplicates
  • What a reinstall does and does not fix
  • Before you start
  • Related

Reinstalling can fix a misbehaving app, and it does not cost you your subscription. Your access belongs to your account details, not to the app.

Keep your details

Before you uninstall, make sure you have your sign-in details saved somewhere private. Without them you will need to ask support to resend.

Clean reinstall

  • Uninstall the app.
  • Restart the device.
  • Install the latest version from the official store.
  • Sign in once and let the channel list and guide load fully.

Avoid duplicates

Adding the same list twice can slow the app and cause confusion. Remove old entries before adding a new one.

What a reinstall does and does not fix

It clears a damaged install, old settings and cached data. It does not fix a weak connection, an expired subscription or a device that is too slow. If the same problem returns within hours, look at the network and the device instead.

Always reinstall the same app from the same store. Installing an unofficial copy to see if it works is the quickest way to create a new problem.

Before you start

Write down the settings you have changed, such as favourites and parental controls, because they may need recreating. Keep your sign-in details to hand.
