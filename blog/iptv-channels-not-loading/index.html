IPTV Channels Not Loading: A Step-by-Step Checklist

When IPTV channels will not load, work through this checklist: connection, app, sign-in details, storage and device restarts, before contacting support.

A black screen or endless spinner has a short list of usual causes. Working through them in order saves time.

1. Check your internet

Open a web page or another app on the same device. If nothing loads, the problem is the connection, not the IPTV service. Restart the router and try again.

2. Check your sign-in details

Confirm that the details match what was sent to you. An extra space or a changed password is a common culprit. If your access has expired, the app may load an empty list.

3. Restart and update

Close the app fully, restart the device and check for an app update. Out-of-date apps can stop working after system updates.

4. Free some space

Low storage can stop an app from saving its channel list. Remove unused apps and clear the app's cache if your device allows it.

5. Try another device

If a phone works but the TV does not, the problem is the TV app or its network, not your subscription.

Single channel or every channel

If only one channel fails, the issue is probably with that stream, not your equipment. Note the channel name and time and tell support. If everything fails, work through connection, sign-in and app checks first.

Date and time on your device

An incorrect date or time can stop an app from connecting securely. Turn on automatic date and time in your device settings, restart and try again.

What to send to support

  • Your device model and app name.
  • What you see: black screen, spinner or an error message.
  • When the problem began and what changed before it.
  • Whether other devices on the same network work.

6. Contact support

Send us the device model, the app name and what you see on screen. A screenshot helps. Our FAQ page covers other common questions.
