IPTV vs Stacking On-Demand Subscriptions: Cost Compared

A method for comparing IPTV with several on-demand subscriptions: list what you watch, add yearly costs and check overlaps before you cancel.

By Wizzo Codes | Published 2 November 2026 | 2 min read

On this page

  • Step 1: list what you actually watch
  • Step 2: add yearly costs
  • Step 3: find overlaps
  • Step 4: check the other costs
  • A worked example
  • Step 5: decide

Subscriptions add up quietly. This method compares them honestly instead of guessing.

Step 1: list what you actually watch

Open each app's history. Write down the shows and live content you used in the past three months.

Step 2: add yearly costs

Multiply each monthly price by twelve. For IPTV, use the total for the length you would choose, such as the twelve month totals on our plans page.

Step 3: find overlaps

If you rarely open an app, it is a candidate to pause. Many on-demand services let you rejoin later.

Step 4: check the other costs

Include a streaming device and broadband. Our guide to 1, 3, 6 or 12 Months? How to Pick an IPTV Subscription Length shows the per-month arithmetic.

A worked example

Suppose you pay for three on-demand apps and open only one weekly. Cancelling the other two saves their full yearly cost. If you want live channels instead, compare that saving with the total for an IPTV plan. The result is simple arithmetic, not a guess.

Keep a note of the date you cancel. Many services allow you to return later, so cancelling is rarely permanent.

Step 5: decide

Keep what you use weekly. Test a replacement with a trial before cancelling anything.
