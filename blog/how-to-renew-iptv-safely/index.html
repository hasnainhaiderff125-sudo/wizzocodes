How to Renew an IPTV Subscription Safely

How to renew safely: check your expiry date, use the same trusted channel, confirm prices and receipts and avoid pressure and look-alike messages.

By Wizzo Codes | Published 2 November 2026 | 2 min read

On this page

  • Know your expiry
  • Use the original contact route
  • Confirm before you pay
  • Warning signs at renewal
  • What we do
  • Keep records

Renewal time is when scammers and mistakes both appear, so a short routine helps.

Know your expiry

Note the date and length you chose when you subscribed. Set a reminder a few days earlier.

Use the original contact route

Contact us through the details on our site or the WhatsApp number you used before. Do not follow links from unknown senders.

Confirm before you pay

Ask for the plan, length and total in writing. Prices are totals for the length chosen. See plans page.

Warning signs at renewal

  • A message from a new number claiming to be us.
  • A link to a payment page you do not recognise.
  • Pressure to pay within minutes.
  • A request for card numbers or passwords.

What we do

We confirm renewals through the same WhatsApp conversation or the contact details on this site, and we confirm the plan and the total before you pay.

Keep records

Save the receipt and the message confirming the new period. See How to Spot IPTV Scams and Pay Safely.
