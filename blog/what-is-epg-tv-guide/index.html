What Is an EPG? The IPTV TV Guide Explained

The EPG is the on-screen programme guide in an IPTV app. Learn how it works, why it sometimes shows the wrong times and how to fix common issues.

By Wizzo Codes | Published 1 November 2026 | 2 min read

  • Why times can be wrong
  • Fixing a missing guide
  • Matching guide data to channels
  • How far ahead should it show

EPG stands for electronic programme guide. It is the grid that shows what is on now and what is coming next, similar to the guide on a traditional set-top box.

Where the data comes from

The guide is not part of the video stream. It is a separate data feed that the player loads and matches to each channel. When the match is wrong or the feed is delayed, you see gaps or the wrong titles.

Why times can be wrong

Time zones are the most common cause. If your device or app is set to the wrong zone, every programme appears shifted. Check that your device is set to UK time and that automatic time is turned on.

Fixing a missing guide

  • Refresh the guide or playlist inside the app.
  • Restart the app and then the device.
  • Make sure the app has time to download the data after first sign-in.
  • Free storage if the device is full, as the guide needs space to save data.

If the problem remains, message support with the app name and a description of what you see.

Matching guide data to channels

Each channel carries an identifier that the guide uses to find its schedule. If the identifier is missing or different, that channel shows no data. Apps often let you refresh or remap the guide, and a missing guide for one channel while the rest work is usually a data issue, not a fault with your connection.

How far ahead should it show

Most guides show a few days of programmes, though this varies by channel. If yours shows only the current programme, try a refresh and give it time to download. Large guides can take a minute on older devices.

Using the guide on match days

For live events, check the guide earlier in the day because schedules can change. Remember that start times in the guide can differ from what you see if the event runs long or is delayed.

Why it is worth having

A working guide makes a large channel list far easier to use, especially for live sport where start times matter. See also Watching Live Sport on IPTV: Preparation Tips for Match Days for how to prepare for match days.
