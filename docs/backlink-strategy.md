# Legitimate authority building plan (UK first, Canada second)

Principle: earn links with useful, accurate material and real relationships. No bought links, no networks, no mass directory submissions.

## 1. Link-worthy assets to create first
| Asset | Why it earns links |
|---|---|
| Streaming speed and Wi-Fi checklist (printable, from /blog/ articles) | Practical, evergreen, useful to broadband and tech bloggers |
| Plain-English IPTV glossary (queued) | Reference page that forums and explainers cite |
| TV Licence and IPTV overview (published) | UK specific, frequently searched, easy to cite when kept factual and neutral |
| "Is my device compatible" checklist (/compatible-devices/) | Useful to device forums and help communities |

## 2. Opportunities
| Opportunity type | Relevance | Geography | Kind | How to approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home networking and broadband help sites | Wi-Fi and speed guides match their audience | UK | Editorial | Offer the Wi-Fi checklist as a resource, with a short factual note on what it adds. One personal email per site |
| Tech and streaming device blogs (Fire TV, Android TV, Apple TV communities) | Setup guides | UK, Canada | Editorial or resource page | Suggest the specific device guide to the owner of a relevant resources page |
| Local business and small business directories (Companies House listing, Google Business Profile only if you have a verifiable business address) | Brand legitimacy | UK | Directory | Claim a handful of reputable listings with accurate, identical name, site and contact details. Skip any that need a fake address |
| Consumer and tech forums and communities | Real answers to real questions | UK, Canada | Community | Answer questions helpfully, disclose your connection, link only when the article truly answers the question. No signature link spam |
| Guest articles on technology or home entertainment sites | Authority and referral traffic | UK, Canada | Editorial | Pitch one specific idea (for example "why evenings are harder for streams") with an outline. Offer unique content, never a rewrite of your own posts |
| Payment and tool partners (listing on partner help pages where it is genuinely appropriate) | Accurate mention of accepted methods | Global | Partnership | Only where the partner already publishes merchant or how-to resources and permits it |
| Press or podcast mentions on streaming habits | Brand mentions | UK | Editorial | Offer an honest data-free commentary or a how-to angle. Avoid claims you cannot prove |

## 3. Process
1. Build 3 assets, then list 30 well matched sites (not 300).
2. Send short, specific, personal emails. No templates with blanks.
3. Track in a sheet: site, contact, date, response, result. Follow up once.
4. Review monthly in Google Search Console: referring pages, new queries, crawl errors.

## 4. Avoid
Paid link packages, private blog networks, comment spam, automated submissions, reviews you have not earned, claims of partnerships with broadcasters or leagues, and anchor text that is all the same exact keyword.
