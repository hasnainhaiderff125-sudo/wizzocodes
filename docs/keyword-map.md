## Cannibalisation controls

- "What is IPTV" belongs only to /blog/iptv-explained/. No new article may target it.
- Troubleshooting articles each own a distinct symptom (buffering overall, Wi-Fi, evening freezing, audio, channels not loading). Future ones must name a new symptom.
- Device articles own one platform each. The overview /blog/iptv-on-phones-tablets-computers/ links to the specific ones.
- Price and plan terms belong to /plans/. Articles link to it rather than repeat full tables.

| /ca/ | IPTV subscription Canada | Canada landing page. hreflang pair with the home page: en-CA <-> en-GB, x-default is the home page |
| /ca/<city>/ (6 pages) | IPTV in Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Ottawa | en-CA, self-referencing canonical, no hreflang pair because there is no matching UK page |

Why "best" is not in the page titles: an unqualified "best" is a claim that cannot be proved and invites trouble with advertising rules. The phrase "best IPTV in <city>" appears naturally in each page's body and keywords, explained honestly as depending on the viewer's setup.

## Cannibalisation controls (local)
- City pages target the city name only. Generic "IPTV subscription UK" stays on the home page and /plans/.
- Canada pages never target UK terms; /ca/ links to the Canada guides.
