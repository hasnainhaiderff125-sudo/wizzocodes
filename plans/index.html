Pricing questions

Before you choose.

More in the full FAQ. Not sure your device works? Read the compatibility checklist or how to choose an IPTV plan.

What is the difference between the plans?

All plans include 24/7 WhatsApp support. The difference is the stated totals for channels, movies and series, which rise from Basic to Ultimate, along with the price.

Are the prices in pounds?

Yes. Prices are in pounds sterling (GBP). Each price is the total for the length you choose, not a monthly price. Where a monthly equivalent is shown, it is for guidance only.

Which plan is most popular?

Premium is marked as most popular.

How do I order a plan?

Choose a length, then press Select on a plan. WhatsApp opens with a message naming the plan, the length and the total price, and we reply with the next steps.

How can I pay?

We accept PayPal, Binance Pay and Wise. We confirm which is easiest for you on WhatsApp.

Can I check it works on my device first?

Yes. Request a free trial on WhatsApp to test channel quality and stability on your own device and connection before you subscribe.
